## What would flip a no

- My Visa getting declined by OpenAI, or a few devs I trust in J2TEAM posting that it's been stable for months with real latency numbers.
- A DeepSeek/Qwen price actually cheaper than going direct, or my card getting blocked for foreign payments.
- If my reseller got shut down or kept serving fake models and I needed a reliable legal source, I'd try it with small top-ups.
- My reseller getting banned or swapping models on me, so I need something legit that won't vanish.
- A public uptime and latency track record of 6+ months that matches OpenRouter, plus proof the refund actually works.
- An independent third party verifying the models are real, plus a track record of a year or more with no outages or shutdown risk.
- A proper service contract with the company's legal details, plus written proof of their provider agreements, or something that shows they're a legitimate reseller.
- Claude available at a real discount, maybe 15,000đ per dollar or under, with the VAT invoice. Then I could take it to accounting and stop worrying about a reseller cutting us off.
- My reseller getting banned or keys dying mid-project, so I need something that stays up, and even then I'd want it under about 5,000đ per dollar.
- Landing a client that needs a valid company VAT invoice and won't accept grey-market credit. Then they'd be paying, not me.
- If my accountant insists on a proper Vietnamese VAT invoice for the company, I'd try the free credit on a side workload, but only with a clear track record and that 30-day refund actually honored.
- If it were clearly cheaper than paying directly, or my card started getting declined, or a client required a Vietnamese VAT invoice for the API costs.
