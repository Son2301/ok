# Product lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **It starts from the experience.** The thesis is built around specific moments: paying, seeing the bill, wondering which model answered, approving a risky agent action, reading a scam verdict. That is the right way to work, from the customer's moment back to the system.
- **It cares about the parts nobody sees.** Receipts in đồng for every action, automatic credit for failed work, spend caps on by default, and copy that never overclaims ("warning signs", never "safe"). A Review bot that stays silent unless a second model fails to refute a finding is restraint, and restraint is taste.
- **One wallet is a real simplification.** VietQR top-up that credits in seconds, credits that never expire, and an automatic e-invoice remove a seam most competitors leave open.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No focus.** Four products serve four different buyers (developers, small teams, shop owners, families) through four first-five-minutes experiences, and a company of one is building all of them. The pitch can't be said in one sentence. "Four AI products on one wallet" is a description of the architecture, not a product. Every seam gets a fraction of the craft that the strategy depends on.
2. **The seams it depends on are not owned or proven.** The promised experience runs through SePay, VietQR, Zalo, GitHub and upstream AI providers, and none of them has been verified live. Anthropic resale is not permitted without approval, and Zalo changed its OA pricing in June 2026. If an approval arrives late on Zalo, or a top-up doesn't credit "in seconds", the "verifiably honest" promise fails at the exact anxious moment it was designed for.
3. **The experience is designed without the customer.** There has been no primary Vietnamese user research and there is no willingness-to-pay data. The anxious moments are hypotheses. Without watching a real shop owner or developer go through the first five minutes, the craft could be spent on the wrong moments.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, what is the one thing, and who is it for?
- If you could ship only one of the four products this year, which would it be, and what would you cut first?
- Walk me through a developer's first five minutes, from landing to first paid call. Where does it hand off between systems, and what happens when SePay or the upstream model fails?
- How many real Vietnamese users have you watched use any part of this? (Open question: none reported.)
- What are the prices, the budget and the runway? (Open questions: none set or stated.)
- Can the gateway work without reselling Anthropic, and is it still the product without that?
- Is free Safe a product, or marketing paid for by gateway margin you haven't measured?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Pick one.** Commit to one product, with wallet + Gateway as the likely candidate, and freeze or drop the other three until it has paying users. State it in one sentence.
2. **Prove the critical seams live.** Run one real VietQR/SePay top-up end to end, one real routed call through a provider whose terms permit resale, and get a receipt in đồng, all verified live.
3. **Put it in front of customers.** Watch 10–20 target users go through the first five minutes, record where they hesitate, and get at least one real price that people actually pay.
4. **Set the numbers.** Prices, budget and runway, written down before more building.
