# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Real pain, sharp moments.** Paying for AI in Vietnam without a card, bill shock, fake models from grey-market resellers (the brief cites an audit of 183 fake "Claude" channels). The pain is concrete and the buyers already spend money on it.
- **The value equation leans on likelihood and effort, which is where offers usually fail.** Top-up by VietQR in seconds and credits that never expire cut effort. A receipt in đồng for every action, authenticity probes and a public incident log raise perceived likelihood. Automatic credit for failed work is the start of a guarantee.
- **Not competing on price.** The pitch is "verifiably the real model, billed honestly in VND", not "cheaper". That is the right stance against cheap resellers.
- **Cheap-to-deliver stack items.** Spend caps, receipts, never-expiring credit and silence-unless-verified review comments cost little and are worth a lot to an anxious buyer.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Four markets, no single offer.** Developers, small shop owners, consumers and businesses buying an API have different pain, money and channels. This lens scores the market first, and right now there are four markets, none tested. No user research or willingness-to-pay data exists. A solo founder spread across four offers sells none of them well.
2. **The core offer may not be legally sellable.** Gateway margin is meant to fund everything, including free Safe. The brief says Anthropic does not permit unapproved resale, and VAT on resold tokens is unresolved. If the headline "real Claude, honestly billed" cannot be sold, the stack loses its anchor and its subsidy.
3. **No proof yet, so likelihood is a promise.** No third-party integration has been verified live: AI providers, SePay, Zalo, GitHub. The whole offer is "verifiably honest", so an unverified product undercuts it at the root. And with no prices set, nothing has been offered to anyone yet.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which one buyer has the most pain and the most money? Name them, and say how you reach the first 20.
- What are the actual prices, and what is the gross margin per product after provider cost, VAT and payment fees?
- What exactly is the guarantee, and can the margin pay for it? "Credit for failed work" is a feature. "If the model isn't the one we said, we refund 10x" is a guarantee.
- Can you sell Claude at all? If not, what does the gateway offer look like without it?
- Could you charge double what grey-market resellers charge and still be the obvious choice? What evidence says buyers pay for authenticity?
- What is the budget and runway, and how long can the gateway subsidise free Safe?
- What is the real, non-fake urgency? The Tết timing for Safe works; what plays that role for the others?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Pick one lead offer, most likely gateway plus wallet for developers. Put Agents and Safe on hold until it pays for itself.
2. Settle resale rights for each provider sold, and the VAT treatment, before launch.
3. Verify the payment path (VietQR/SePay) and at least one provider live. Publish the authenticity probe results as proof.
4. Set real prices and run a willingness-to-pay test with 10–20 named prospects. At least some must prepay.
5. Write a named guarantee with a cost cap the margin can cover.
