# Monopoly lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **There is a secret, and it is specific.** Most people treat Vietnam's AI resale market as a price game. The brief says it is a trust game: grey-market resellers sell cheap credit, and an audit found 183 fake "Claude" channels. "We prove which model answered" is a contrarian claim that can be checked, and the large incumbents are not fighting on it.
- **The counter-positioning is real.** Credits that never expire, spend caps on by default and automatic refunds for failed work are hard for incumbents to copy when they profit from breakage and metered surprises.
- **The right local rails.** A VND wallet over VietQR/SePay plus a Vietnamese e-invoice cuts friction that card-first global players will not bother to remove. That is a small market they ignore, which is the kind of market this lens wants to start in.
- **The distribution ideas are built into the product.** PR footers from Review and Safe share cards before Tết spread through use, not paid ads.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No technology ten times better, and the core is resale.** Gateway resells other companies' models. Anthropic does not permit unapproved resale, and suppliers can cut access or reprice whenever they like. Honest receipts and VietQR top-ups are easy improvements for a funded local rival to copy within six months. If the supply side can switch the business off, nothing can be monopolised.
2. **Four markets, no small one owned.** Gateway, Agents, Safe and Review each face a named, focused competitor (OpenRouter, Lindy, CodeRabbit, Chống Lừa Đảo, plus Gemini's Android warnings and a nonprofit Zalo bot). A solo founder spreading effort across four products is unlikely to win a dominant share of any of them. Gateway margin paying for free Safe ties the weakest unit economics to the riskiest supply.
3. **Distribution is unproven and unpriced.** There is no user research, no willingness-to-pay data and no CAC, and no third-party API has been verified live. The brief does not say how the first 100 paying customers arrive or what each one costs.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which one small market do you plan to own first, and what share of it in 12 months? How many buyers are in it? (The size is not known.)
- What is the secret only you hold? Is it the fake-model audit, a supply relationship, or something else?
- What happens to Gateway if Anthropic or another provider refuses resale? Is there a fully licensed supply path?
- What would a funded Vietnamese competitor be unable to copy in month six? Is the authenticity-probe record a real data asset, or a feature?
- Where do the first 100 paying customers come from, and what does each one cost?
- What is the runway, and what are the actual prices and margins?
- Is there a network effect anywhere, for example scam reports making Safe better? Would PDPL allow it?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Pick one wedge and park the rest.** That means verified-authentic gateway access for Vietnamese developers, or Review on its own. Agents and paid Safe wait.
2. **Get licensed supply in writing** from at least one major model provider before taking customer money for its models.
3. **Prove it live:** SePay and at least one provider verified end to end, and a first cohort of paying customers with a measured CAC and margin.
4. **Make the authenticity record a moat:** published probes run on a schedule and built up over time into a dataset no one else has, so honesty becomes a brand that is hard to copy, not a slogan.
5. **Clear the legal questions** (VAT on resold tokens, PDPL for blacklists) before scaling.
