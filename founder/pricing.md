# Pricing: Minh AI Gateway

Unit: đồng charged per US$1 of official provider price. Sources: [pricing-curve.md](pricing-curve.md) (100 simulated buyers), competitor fees as noted. There is no `numbers.json` yet, so margins below are worked by hand, not from the CFO tool.

## 1. The price: 27,000đ per US$1 (≈3.8% over a 26,000đ bank rate)

- **Panel range:** acceptable 22,979–27,973đ; least resistance (OPP) ≈23,000đ; indifference (IPP) ≈25,520đ. 27,000đ sits inside the range, near the top.
- **Buyers vs passers:** the 61 buyers' median "too expensive" is 29,500đ; the 39 passers' is 27,500đ. Price is not what divides them; the passers are mostly grey-market users no price inside the range will win.
- **Against substitutes (estimates, check before publishing):**
  - Card direct to the provider: bank rate plus a foreign-transaction fee of roughly 2–3.5% ≈ 26,500–26,900đ, with no usable VAT invoice.
  - OpenRouter: about 5.5% top-up fee on top of the card cost ≈ 27,400đ+.
  - Grey-market resellers: the strategy report puts them near 1,600đ. Unverified, and not the market to chase.
- **Why not 26,000đ (the pitch's 0% fee):** at the bank rate the margin is roughly zero before payment fees, e-invoice cost, failed-request credits, refunds and the free 50,000đ. Unresolved VAT on resold tokens could turn it negative. That is selling below variable cost; nothing in the board or panel says the business can afford it.
- **Margin at 27,000đ:** about 1,000đ per US$1 gross (≈3.7% of revenue) before those costs. Thin. `/founder-cfo` must confirm it covers them; if not, the range allows up to ~27,900đ.

## 2. The ladder

One meter, priced per dollar, with rungs that buy trust, not tokens:

| rung | price | who | reason to step up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dev | 27,000đ/$, prepaid VietQR | freelancers, side projects | — |
| Team | 27,000đ/$ + monthly VAT e-invoice batch, team keys, per-key caps | startup and agency devs | one invoice a month, shared wallet |
| Company | 26,500đ/$ on top-ups of 20M đ+, signed service contract, named support | SME CTOs (84% buy rate) | lower rate, contract and proof of resale rights the panel asked for |

The volume discount is real, not an anchor: it is the only price below 27,000đ.

## 3. Opening offer

Keep the 50,000đ free credit. Add, until **31 January 2027**: the first 100 company accounts get the Company rate on any top-up size. No "was" prices.

## 4. What to test with real buyers

Two prices inside the range: **26,500đ vs 27,500đ**. Two landing pages with a VietQR pre-pay (a real refundable top-up), sent to the same mix of J2TEAM/dev-group posts and 20 named SME CTOs. Measure paid top-ups, not sign-ups.

## 5. Price objections, verbatim (for marketing)

1. "I already pay about 1,600đ per dollar of usage through my Telegram reseller, so paying 26,000đ for the same dollar makes no sense for my side projects." (P014)
2. "Same official price plus your exchange rate gives me nothing new except paying by QR, and I don't need a VAT invoice as a freelancer." (P011)
3. "At full official price I'm not moving my main usage off grey-market keys that cost me half." (P007)

These buyers pass on legitimacy, not on 1,000đ. Answer with the invoice, contract and authenticity record, not a lower price.
