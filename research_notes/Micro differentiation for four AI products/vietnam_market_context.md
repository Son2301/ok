# Vietnam Market Context for Selling AI Software (Dev Tools, SMB Automation, Consumer Safety) — Micro-Level Details, as of Oct 2026

> Research-method note for the report writer: research was done 25 Oct 2026 via web search only. Direct page fetches failed for almost every .vn domain and many law-firm sites (DNS/egress blocks), and the shared web-search budget ran out partway through. So many findings below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not full-text reads. Where a figure was only seen in a snippet or a URL slug, it is flagged. Items I believe to be true but could not source are kept out of Cited Findings and listed under Gaps as "unverified leads".

## 1. Payments: rails, foreign-card friction, foreign-digital-service tax, VAT and e-invoices

### Takeaway
Vietnamese buyers pay through bank-app QR transfer (VietQR on NAPAS), not cards. About 90M mobile-banking accounts scan VietQR, against an estimated 14–16M credit cards. Local reconciliation services (SePay, payOS) let a solo founder take VietQR payments with automatic order confirmation for free or for about 99k VND/month. On the business side, the tax and invoice rules give a local seller with VN invoices a structural edge. The 2025–2026 rules push tax withholding onto banks for foreign digital suppliers that have not registered (new Circular 90/2026, effective 1 Jul 2026). Software is VAT-exempt under the new VAT Law, and the e-invoice rules were tightened again from 1 Jun 2025 (Decree 70/2025).

NAPAS planned QR links with Thailand, Cambodia, Laos (done) and China via UnionPay (Dec 2025). — [NAPAS 2025 member conference](https://en.napas.com.vn/napas-2025-member-organization-conference-184260317124736875.htm)

**Tax on foreign digital services (affects the price of global competitors)**
- Law 56/2024/QH15 amended Art. 42 of the Tax Administration Law, effective 1 Jan 2025. Foreign suppliers doing e-commerce, digital-platform or other cross-border business must register, declare and pay tax in Vietnam, directly or through an authorized party. — [Thị trường Tài chính Tiền tệ (Banking Association)](https://thitruongtaichinhtiente.vn/hiep-hoi-ngan-hang-kien-nghi-thao-go-vuong-mac-trong-quy-dinh-ngan-hang-thuong-mai-khau-tru-nop-thay-nghia-vu-thue-voi-nha-cung-cap-nuoc-ngoai-66044.html)
- The tax authority asked commercial banks to withhold and pay tax on payments to unregistered foreign suppliers. Banks pushed back: a transfer order carries no website or recipient-type information, and Vietcombank called bank withholding "infeasible and not in line with international practice". — [Diễn đàn Doanh nghiệp](https://diendandoanhnghiep.vn/ngan-hang-gap-kho-khi-phai-nop-thue-thay-nha-cung-cap-nuoc-ngoai-10151480.html); [DNSE](https://www.dnse.com.vn/senses/tin-tuc/ngan-hang-kien-nghi-bo-quy-dinh-nop-thue-thay-nha-thau-nuoc-ngoai-35096116)
- **New in 2026:** Circular 90/2026/TT-BTC (issued 30 Jun 2026, effective 1 Jul 2026) governs tax registration. It defines "foreign supplier" by reference to Decree 252/2026/NĐ-CP. A foreign supplier need not register for tax if all of its Vietnam revenue has already been withheld and paid by banks or payment intermediaries. The previous Circular 86/2024/TT-BTC had no such exemption. — [MISA meInvoice: TT90/2026 PDF](https://www.meinvoice.vn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/TT90-2026-Dang-ky-thue.pdf); [thue.man.net.vn analysis](https://thue.man.net.vn/en/?p=7045) (secondary; I could not open the official text)
- The foreign-supplier tax portal has been live since 21 Mar 2022. Older figures: 42 foreign suppliers paying about 1,900 billion VND, and separately 26 organizations paying USD 20M through the portal (both older reports with no 2026 update). — [ThuVienPhapLuat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/ma-so-thue/phap-luat-thue/nha-cung-cap-o-nuoc-ngoai-thuc-hien-khai-thue-nhu-the-nao-433497-190054.html); [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/42-nha-cung-cap-nuoc-ngoai-hoat-dong-xuyen-bien-gioi-tu-giac-nop-thue-o-viet-nam.htm); [Thời báo Tài chính](https://thoibaotaichinhvietnam.vn/26-to-chuc-nop-20-trieu-usd-tien-thue-qua-cong-thong-tin-dien-tu-danh-cho-nha-cung-cap-nuoc-ngoai-110099.html)
- No source confirmed whether OpenAI or Anthropic is registered on the VN foreign-supplier portal. — (search returned nothing; see Gaps)

**VAT on software**
- Under the new VAT Law 48/2024/QH15 (effective 1 Jul 2025), Art. 5(21), software products and software services are "not subject to VAT" (đối tượng không chịu thuế), "in accordance with law". Decree 181/2025/NĐ-CP details the exempt software products and services. Decree 353/2025/NĐ-CP (from 1 Jan 2026) defines "software product" under the Digital Technology Industry Law. Tax-authority guidance includes Official Letter 2965/CT-CS (May 2026). — [MISA SME: bán phần mềm có chịu thuế GTGT không](https://sme.misa.vn/345925/ban-phan-mem-co-chiu-thue-gtgt-khong/); [ThuVienPhapLuat: CV 2965/CT-CS](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/ma-so-thue/phap-luat-thue/cong-van-2965ctcs-ve-thue-suat-thue-gtgt-doi-voi-san-pham-phan-mem-va-dich-vu-phan-mem-224866.html); [ThuVienPhapLuat: dịch vụ phần mềm](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/ma-so-thue/phap-luat-thue/dich-vu-phan-mem-gom-nhung-loai-nao-dich-vu-phan-mem-co-phai-la-doi-tuong-chiu-thue-gtgt-khong-379725-186984.html)
- The Vietnamese press is already covering whether businesses that buy AI applications and resell them domestically owe tax. This is relevant to "rebadged API reseller" models. Only the headline was seen. — [Báo Pháp luật Doanh nhân](https://doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn/doanh-nghiep-mua-ung-dung-tri-tue-nhan-tao-va-ban-lai-o-trong-nuoc-co-phai-chiu-thue.html)

**E-invoices (hóa đơn điện tử)**
- Decree 70/2025/NĐ-CP was issued 20 Mar 2025 and took effect 1 Jun 2025, amending Decree 123/2020. It extends cash-register-generated e-invoices to household and individual businesses with annual revenue of 1 billion VND or more, and to enterprises selling directly to consumers. It also revises invoice-timing rules (including for services and recurring or high-volume sales), lets foreign suppliers register to use e-invoices via the tax portal, and adds grounds for suspending a taxpayer's e-invoice use. — [ThuVienPhapLuat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/hoi-dap-phap-luat/diem-moi-ve-dang-ky-su-dung-hoa-don-dien-tu-theo-nghi-dinh-70-138046958.html); [Kế toán An Pha](https://ketoananpha.vn/nhung-diem-moi-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-tu-01-06-2025.html); [MISA meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/31439/nghi-dinh-70-2025-nd-cp-sua-doi-nghi-dinh-123/); [Sapo blog](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/nghi-dinh-so-70-2025-nd-cp)
- Circular 32/2025/TT-BTC replaced Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC on e-invoices from 1 Jun 2025. — [MISA meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/31439/nghi-dinh-70-2025-nd-cp-sua-doi-nghi-dinh-123/); [einvoice.vn on CV 348/CT-CS](https://einvoice.vn/tin-tuc/cong-van-348-ct-cs-nhung-diem-moi-quan-trong-tai-nghi-dinh-70-2025-nd-cp)
- Sources conflict on erroneous invoices: one says Decree 70 adds rules for cancelling erroneous tax-coded invoices not yet sent to the buyer; another says it removes the rule on cancelling wrongly issued invoices. — [ihoadon](https://ihoadon.vn/hddt/diem-moi-can-chu-y-cua-nghi-dinh-70-ve-hoa-don-chung-tu.html) vs [ketoananpha](https://ketoananpha.vn/nhung-diem-moi-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-tu-01-06-2025.html)

### Inferences
- **"Chuyển khoản / quét mã QR" must be the default checkout, not a card form.** About 90M accounts scan VietQR while credit cards number roughly 14–16M, so a Stripe-only checkout excludes most buyers. A solo founder can ship VietQR with exact-amount, unique-memo codes and SePay/payOS webhooks in a day, then show "Đã nhận thanh toán" within seconds. That instant confirmation itself builds trust.
- **Pay-as-you-go top-ups ("nạp tiền") fit the rails.** Bank transfers cannot auto-renew the way card subscriptions do, so prepaid wallets, credit packs and "gia hạn" reminders over Zalo/email fit VietQR better than auto-billing. This is an inference from how the rails work, not a measured preference.
- **"Xuất được hóa đơn" is a B2B moat against OpenAI, Anthropic, GitHub and Cursor.** A Vietnamese business that pays a foreign SaaS by card gets no Vietnamese e-invoice. Withholding responsibility also sits with banks and remains contested. A local company that issues a compliant e-invoice (software is VAT-exempt, so the invoice line shows "KCT", not 10%) gives accountants a clean, deductible expense document. I did not find a source that states the CIT deductibility rule, so verify it (see Gaps).
- **A VN reseller of Claude/OpenAI must think about its tax position,** because the press is already asking whether AI-app resale is taxed differently. Selling as "dịch vụ phần mềm" from a VN entity is likely the cleaner structure, but this needs a tax adviser.
- **Bank-partner co-marketing is a cheap channel.** SePay's VPBank, BIDV and Vietcombank promos show that VN banks subsidize SME tooling to win business accounts.

### Gaps
- No official SBV credit-card count. The 14–16M figure is an unsourced estimate.
- Exact withholding rates that banks apply on payments to unregistered foreign suppliers. My unverified lead: under Circular 80/2021 deemed rates, services are about 5% VAT + 5% CIT. I could not confirm how Circular 90/2026 and Decree 252/2026 operate, or whether individual card payments to OpenAI, Anthropic or GitHub are actually being withheld in 2026.
- Whether OpenAI, Anthropic, GitHub or Cursor are registered on the VN foreign-supplier portal, or issue VN-compliant invoices.
- Whether GitHub/Copilot and Cursor still decline VN cards in 2026. The only decline evidence is from 2023.
- payOS and Casso pricing, and confirmation that payOS is operated by Casso (I believe it is, unverified). Pages were unreachable.
- MoMo, ZaloPay and VNPay user counts and merchant fees for 2025–2026. Not retrieved before the search budget ran out.
- Unverified leads to check: (a) SBV Decision 2345/QĐ-NHNN, which since 1 Jul 2024 has required biometric authentication for online transfers above 10M VND per transaction or 20M VND per day. This adds friction to large B2B payments, so price tiers under 10M/transaction are smoother. (b) Most banks waive fees on interbank Napas 247 transfers. (c) The CIT rule that expenses need lawful invoices/documents to be deductible, and how it applies to foreign-SaaS card receipts.

## 2. Pricing psychology: VND anchors, subscription vs pay-per-use, trials, CTV/affiliate and resellers

### Takeaway
Local SaaS anchors on round "k" prices per month: 50k, 99k, 199k, 270k, 330k, 490k. Local tools usually have free tiers sized for tiny shops, such as SePay's 50 transactions/month. For AI specifically, the strongest observable price signal is the large grey market of cheap shared and resold ChatGPT accounts. VN consumers will pay, but at a fraction of USD 20, and they are exposed to scams. I found no quantitative survey on subscription vs one-time ("mua đứt") preference or on CTV commission norms.

### Cited Findings
- KiotViet (market-leading retail POS): recent reviews list Hỗ Trợ at 270,000đ/month, Chuyên Nghiệp at 330,000đ/month and Cao Cấp at 490,000đ/month, possibly excluding VAT. An older official notice (about 1.5 years old) listed Chuyên nghiệp at 310,000đ/month. Add-on branch/warehouse fees conflict between sources: 150k per warehouse vs 270k per branch. — [POS365 review of KiotViet](https://www.pos365.vn/review-phan-mem-kiotviet-tinh-nang-uu-nhuoc-diem-bang-gia-8613.html); [MOR Software 2026 list](https://vn.morsoftware.com/blog/phan-mem-quan-ly-kho-mien-phi); [KiotViet price-adjustment notice](https://www.kiotviet.vn/kiotviet-thong-bao-dieu-chinh-gia-ban-san-pham-phan-mem-quan-ly-ban-hang/); [Quasa: Sapo vs KiotViet](https://quasa.io/vi/media/sapo-hay-kiotviet-ban-da-kenh-co-the-dang-them-phi-hon-gia-goi-re)
- SePay price ladder: free (50 tx/month), 99,000đ SHOP, and eShop at 50,000 / 99,000 / 199,000đ per month. Overage is billed per transaction rather than cutting off service ("mọi chức năng vẫn hoạt động bình thường"). — [SePay bảng giá](https://sepay.vn/bang-gia.html); [SePay eShop](https://sepay.vn/sepay-eshop.html)
- Grey market for AI: shared ChatGPT accounts ("tài khoản dùng chung") are described as the most popular form of cheap ChatGPT in Vietnam. Speed and stability depend on the number of co-users, and chat-history privacy is at risk. — [Hoàng Hà Mobile](https://hoanghamobile.com/tin-tuc/?p=521429) (snippet-level); [Chiaki listing selling cheap ChatGPT Plus](https://chiaki.vn/tai-khoan-chatgpt-plus-gia-re) (a seller, shown as evidence the market exists)
- The press has reported the risk of "losing money, low-quality product" in the ChatGPT account market and a booming market for buying, selling and sharing accounts. Dates are uncertain, likely 2023–2024. — [VietnamFinance](https://vietnamfinance.vn/rui-ro-mat-tien-san-pham-kem-chat-luong-tren-thi-truong-tai-khoan-chatgpt-d92354.html); [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/techconnect/no-ro-cho-mua-ban-chia-se-tai-khoan-chatgpt.htm)
- e-Conomy SEA 2025: 81% of Vietnamese respondents interact with AI tools/features every day, and Vietnam ranks first in SEA for AI engagement and trust. Demand exists, so willingness to pay at local price points is the question. — [Vietstock](https://en.vietstock.vn/2025/11/vietnams-digital-economy-to-touch-39-billion-in-2025-38-625181.htm)
- Generic VN coverage frames SaaS as "thuê phần mềm" (renting software) paid monthly or yearly, and calls SaaS still "a fairly new term in Vietnam", naming Base.vn, MISA and 1Office. The article is older. — [VietTimes](https://viettimes.vn/vi-sao-cac-doanh-nghiep-phai-chuyen-tu-saap-sang-saas-post95832.html); [Thế Giới Di Động explainer](https://www.thegioididong.com/hoi-dap/saas-la-gi-so-sanh-saas-paas-va-iaas-1587494)

### Inferences
- **Price in VND with "k" anchors and a sub-100k entry tier.** Global AI tools at USD 20 (about 500k+ VND) sit above KiotViet's top tier. A local dev tool or SMB agent at 99k–199k/month, or a credit pack at 50k/100k/200k, matches what SMB owners already pay for core business software.
- **Never hard-cut on overage.** SePay keeps service running and bills the overage later, which signals reliability to shop owners whose sales depend on the tool.
- **Turn the shared-account grey market into a pitch.** Individual accounts at the price of a shared one, with no co-users and private history, attack the main weakness of ChatGPT resellers. "Tài khoản riêng, không dùng chung" and "hóa đơn đầy đủ" are credible differentiators.
- **Annual prepay.** KiotViet-style pricing is typically quoted per month but billed in multi-month blocks (unverified, see Gaps). Offering 6- or 12-month bank-transfer prepay with a bonus month fits a QR-transfer world.

### Gaps
- No survey data on SMB preference for "mua đứt" (one-time purchase) vs subscription, on lifetime-deal culture, or on trial-length norms.
- No sourced data on CTV/affiliate commission norms (my unverified lead: 20–40% recurring for software CTVs is common) or on how KiotViet, Sapo and MISA reseller and agency networks are paid.
- KiotViet's minimum billing period and trial length could not be confirmed (official page unreachable).
- Price points for the major VN AI chat products (e.g., Zalo AI, Kiki, FPT AI) were not retrieved.

## 3. Distribution and trust: Zalo, Facebook groups, TikTok, community forums, founder face, support expectations, AI-scam skepticism

### Takeaway
Vietnam is a messaging-and-groups market. Zalo has roughly 77–80M MAU and about 2B messages/day, with 155k+ businesses on it as of 2024. Facebook's addressable audience is 79–88M, with TikTok the fastest-growing platform. Trust is the binding constraint: online-fraud losses were 6,000–8,000+ billion VND in 2025 (after more than 12,000 billion in 2024), and officials named AI/deepfake fraud a top method. Cheap ChatGPT "upgrade" and shared-account scams have specifically trained users to distrust AI resellers. A real, reachable local founder and a compliant invoice therefore count as trust signals.

### Cited Findings
**Reach**
- DataReportal Digital 2026: 85.6M internet users at end-2025 (84.2% penetration) and 79.0M social-media user identities in Oct 2025 (77.6% of the population). — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Vietnam](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-vietnam)
- Platform figures are secondary and indicative. Facebook about 79M users and Zalo 78.3M MAU, with TikTok growing fastest at +9.9% YoY, per Elite Asia. NapoleonCat puts Facebook's addressable ad audience at 88.5M in May 2026 (85.2% of the population). — [Elite Asia](https://www.eliteasia.co/?p=37347); [NapoleonCat](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/facebook-users-in-viet_nam/2026/05/)

Znews headline: "nearly 80 million Zalo users per month in 2025" (slug only, body not read). — [Znews](https://znews.vn/gan-80-trieu-nguoi-dung-zalo-moi-thang-trong-nam-2025-post1623499.html)

### Inferences
- **Zalo is the support and retention channel; Facebook groups and TikTok are acquisition.** Running a Zalo OA plus a Zalo support group per customer cohort, with a named founder answering, is the cheapest way to look "real". Government agencies also run OAs, so a verified OA borrows institutional familiarity.
- **Consumer-safety apps have a large, documented pain:** 6,000–12,000 billion VND lost per year, low reporting rates (about 32%), and deepfakes named as a top threat. The Tết period shows seasonal spikes. Content timed to Tết scam waves, and reports that help users file police reports, are natural hooks.
- **Pre-empt the "AI reseller scam" reflex.** Never ask for card photos or OTPs, show a company tax code (MST) and an e-invoice, publish the founder's face and name, and explain clearly what model or API sits underneath. Being transparent about the model is the opposite of the rebadged-API grey market.

- KOL/TikTok creator pricing for B2B software was not retrieved.
- Free government anti-scam tools that compete with consumer-safety apps were not verified (unverified leads: the NCA/Ministry "nTrust" app, chongluadao.vn, and VNeID/MPS warning features).
- Unverified lead: Decree 147/2024/NĐ-CP requires social-network accounts to be authenticated by Vietnamese phone number or VNeID (from 25 Dec 2024). This matters if a consumer app has any user-generated or social features.

## 4. Regulation: AI Law, Digital Technology Industry Law, PDPL, Decree 13/53, new Cybersecurity Law, data localization

### Takeaway
2026 is the first year of a full stack of new laws. These are the Digital Technology Industry Law (71/2025, effective 1 Jan 2026), the PDPL (91/2025) with Decree 356/2025 (both effective 1 Jan 2026, replacing Decree 13/2023), the standalone AI Law (134/2025, effective 1 Mar 2026) with guiding Decree 142/2026 (effective 1 May 2026), a PDPL sanctions decree (330/2026, effective 19 Aug 2026), and the consolidated Cybersecurity Law (116/2025, effective 1 Jul 2026). For a solo founder, the practical obligations are: AI-interaction disclosure and machine-readable labels on AI-generated media, consent-based data processing, and a cross-border-transfer impact assessment if user data goes to US LLM APIs. Startups get a 5-year grace period on DPO/DPIA unless they process sensitive or large-volume data.

### Cited Findings
**AI Law 134/2025/QH15**
- Passed 10 Dec 2025 at the 10th session of the 15th National Assembly, with 90.70% approval. Effective 1 Mar 2026; 35 articles; the guiding philosophy is "manage to develop". — [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/luat-tri-tue-nhan-tao-se-co-hieu-luc-tu-ngay-132026.htm); [VOH](https://voh.com.vn/xa-hoi/luat-tri-tue-nhan-tao-co-hieu-luc-2026-viet-nam-hoan-thien-khung-quan-ly-ai-640627.html)
- Creates a National AI Development Fund, an "AI voucher" mechanism and a controlled sandbox. AI-generated audio, image and video must carry machine-readable identification. Providers must ensure users know when they are interacting with an AI system. An expert said existing AI systems in health, education and finance get 18 months to comply. — [VOH](https://voh.com.vn/xa-hoi/luat-tri-tue-nhan-tao-co-hieu-luc-2026-viet-nam-hoan-thien-khung-quan-ly-ai-640627.html); [RMIT Vietnam](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/vi/tin-tuc/tat-ca-tin-tuc/2026/jan/nam-bat-co-hoi-tu-luat-tri-tue-nhan-tao-moi-ban-hanh); [Apolat Legal: prohibited acts from 1 Mar 2026](https://apolatlegal.com/vi/laws/luat-tri-tue-nhan-tao-2025-so-134-2025-qh15/)
- The law is risk-based and draws heavily on the EU AI Act. High-risk systems need pre-market conformity assessment (for systems on a list the Prime Minister will issue), risk management, human oversight, and registration on a national AI portal. Foreign providers of high-risk systems subject to pre-market assessment must have a commercial presence or authorized representative in VN; other high-risk foreign providers need a lawful local contact point. Summaries differ on whether labeling and transparency duties fall mainly on medium-risk systems (One Asia) or on all providers (Securiti). — [Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/new-law-on-artificial-intelligence-takes-effect-on-1-march-2026/); [Duane Morris](https://blogs.duanemorris.com/vietnam/2026/03/03/vietnam-the-first-law-on-artificial-intelligence-what-you-must-know/); [Securiti](https://securiti.ai/vietnam-ai-aw/); [One Asia](https://oneasia.legal/en/wp-content/themes/standard_black_cmspro/img/7f096307e11079d35cf4753c22d38b33.pdf)
- Decree 142/2026/NĐ-CP (issued 30 Apr 2026, effective 1 May 2026) is the first detailed AI-law guidance. As of June 2026, the PM's high-risk list, conformity-assessment methods and the national AI one-stop portal were still pending. — [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/vietnam-takes-major-step-in-ai-governance-with-new-guiding-decree/9/)

**Digital Technology Industry Law 71/2025/QH15**
- Effective 1 Jan 2026; its financial and investment incentive provisions took effect early, on 1 Jul 2025. It sets seven core AI principles (human-centric, ethical, transparent, explainable, controllable, lifecycle risk management). Specific incentives depend on later decrees. — [Lex Mundi AI guide](https://www.lexmundi.com/guides/ai-legislative-guide/jurisdictions/asia-pacific/vietnam/); [Tilleke](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/vietnams-digital-tech-industry-law-building-the-future-of-ai-chips-and-digital-assets/7/); [Text on ThuVienPhapLuat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Cong-nghe-thong-tin/Law-71-2025-QH15-Digital-Technology-Industry-668801.aspx)

**Personal Data Protection Law 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025**
- The PDPL took effect 1 Jan 2026. Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP (issued 31 Dec 2025, effective 1 Jan 2026) replaces Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP. It lists basic and sensitive personal data and sets consent requirements and cross-border transfer conditions. — [EY](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/nghi-dinh-so-356-2025-nd-cp-quy-dinh-chi-tiet-mot-so-dieu-va-bien-phap-thi-hanh-luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan); [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/vn/vn/publications/legal-news-brief/20260128-new-rules-personal-data-protection.html); [ThuVienPhapLuat text](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Quyen-dan-su/Nghi-dinh-356-2025-ND-CP-huong-dan-Luat-Bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-687428.aspx)
- Startups and small enterprises have a 5-year grace period from the effective date before they must file impact assessments or appoint a DPO. This does not apply if they are data-processing service providers or process sensitive or large-volume data. Micro-enterprises and household businesses are exempt. — [Conventus Law](https://conventuslaw.com/report/vietnams-new-personal-data-protection-law-what-businesses-need-to-know/); [Vietnam Briefing](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnam-law-on-personal-data-protection-latest-developments-and-insights.html/); [KPMG](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/vn/pdf/2025/07/exploring-vietnam-personal-data-protection-law.pdf)
- Cross-border transfers need an assessment submitted to the Ministry of Public Security within 60 days. Fines for cross-border breaches reach 5% of prior-year revenue (or a VND 3 billion floor or cap, depending on the source). Buying or selling personal data can be fined up to 10x the revenue gained or VND 3 billion, whichever is higher. — [Conventus Law](https://conventuslaw.com/report/vietnams-new-personal-data-protection-law-what-businesses-need-to-know/); [UniConsent](https://www.uniconsent.com/vietnam-pdpl); [Roedl](https://www.roedl.com/insights/newsflash-asean/2025_03/vietnam-personal-data-protection-law-passed)
- Decree 330/2026/NĐ-CP on administrative sanctions for personal-data violations took effect 19 Aug 2026, with a maximum fine of VND 3 billion. Single source; no dedicated sanctions decree existed before that date. — [Luật Việt An](https://luatvietan.vn/nghi-dinh-330-2026-nd-cp.html)
- Data-protection personnel must have at least a college degree and 2 years of relevant experience (per a Decree 356 summary). — [PwC PDF](https://www.pwc.com/vn/vn/publications/2026/20260128-new-rules-personal-data-protection.pdf)

**Data localization**
- Decree 53/2022/NĐ-CP (issued 15 Aug 2022, effective 1 Oct 2022) implements the 2018 Cybersecurity Law. Foreign enterprises in telecom, domain, e-commerce, online payments, social networks, online games and similar sectors must store data in VN and set up a branch or representative office when the Minister of Public Security requests it. Reported specifics are at least 24 months of storage and 12 months to comply. The business may choose its storage form. US business groups called the wording vague. — [Deloitte](https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/vn/Documents/tax/vn-tax-alert-decree-53-vi.pdf); [RFA](https://www.rfa.org/vietnamese/news/vietnamnews/us-business-groups-says-vietnam-data-rules-a-significant-burden-09092022084550.html); [LSVN](https://lsvn.vn/tu-01102022-du-lieu-nguoi-dung-internet-tai-viet-nam-se-duoc-luu-tru1661052929.html)
- The new Cybersecurity Law 116/2025/QH15 passed 10 Dec 2025 (434/443 votes, 91.75%) and took effect 1 Jul 2026. It merges the 2015 Network Information Security Law and the 2018 Cybersecurity Law. MPS published a draft guiding decree on 13 Feb 2026, and the search found no final version. — [Hà Tĩnh portal](https://hatinh.gov.vn/vi/bai-viet/luat-an-ninh-mang-co-hieu-luc-tu-172026-dat-con-nguoi-o-vi-tri-trung-tam); [ThuVienPhapLuat text](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Cong-nghe-thong-tin/Luat-An-ninh-mang-2025-so-116-2025-QH15-666020.aspx); [CaseLaw.vn overview](https://caselaw.vn/bai-viet/toan-canh-khung-phap-ly-ve-linh-vuc-cong-nghe-thong-tin-tai-viet-nam-giai-doan-2024-2026-va-luu-y-danh-cho-doanh-nghiep-co-von-dau-tu-nuoc-ngoai-phan-1-3)
- Social networks must remove violating content within 3–6 hours in urgent cases (URL slug only). — [LSVN](https://lsvn.vn/mang-xa-hoi-buoc-go-noi-dung-vi-pham-trong-03-06-gio-doi-voi-truong-hop-khan-cap-a167670.html)

### Inferences
- **Compliance as a feature.** A local founder can ship "Tuân thủ Luật BVDLCN 91/2025 & Luật AI 134/2025" as visible product features. Examples: a consent screen in Vietnamese, an "AI-generated" label on outputs, a per-workspace "data stays in Vietnam" toggle (local DB plus optional local LLM), and a downloadable DPIA/cross-border assessment template for SMB customers. Global tools are unlikely to localize these.
- **Hosting in Vietnam is a soft trust signal, not a legal requirement for most small SaaS.** Decree 53 localization applies on MPS request to listed sectors, but the PDPL cross-border assessment applies whenever personal data leaves VN, for example prompts containing customer data sent to US LLM APIs. Offering VN-hosted storage and redacting PII before calling foreign LLMs removes friction for SMB buyers.
- **Consumer-safety apps must label any AI-generated audio or video and disclose AI interaction.** An app that detects deepfakes or scam calls should itself display the AI-disclosure notice under Art. 11-type rules.
- **Watch for the PM's high-risk AI list.** Credit scoring, recruitment screening and health uses are the likely categories by EU analogy (inference). SMB automation that touches hiring or lending decisions should avoid becoming "high-risk" until the list is published.

### Gaps
- The official full text of the AI Law, Decree 142/2026, Decree 356/2025 and Decree 330/2026 could not be read; all findings are from secondary summaries.
- Data-localization specifics in the new Cybersecurity Law 116/2025 and its implementing decree, and whether Decree 53/2022 has been replaced.
- Local data-center and cloud pricing and capacity (Viettel IDC, FPT Smart Cloud, VNG Cloud, CMC) were not retrieved before the search budget ran out.
- Concrete AI-voucher amounts and eligibility under the AI Law and Decree 142.

## 5. Language and tone: UX writing norms, diacritics, machine-translated UIs, Vietnamese LLM quality

### Takeaway
Little citable material exists on Vietnamese UX-writing norms (pronoun choice, diacritics); this area mostly rests on practitioner knowledge, flagged below as unverified. On model quality, the local ecosystem is active. Zalo AI and universities released the VMLU benchmark suite (ACL 2025) evaluating GPT-4, Qwen2.5 and Llama-3 in Vietnamese. Viettel announced a 120B "sovereign" Vietnamese model (June 2026) built on NVIDIA Nemotron, and Zalo joined the Âu Lạc AI Alliance (June 2025). VinAI's PhởGPT dates from 2023.

### Cited Findings
- VMLU is described as the first Vietnamese Multitask Language Understanding benchmark suite, with four datasets covering general knowledge, reading comprehension, reasoning and conversation. It was published at ACL 2025 by a team mostly from Zalo AI and Vietnamese universities. It evaluates Llama-3, Qwen2.5 and GPT-4, finds that prompt design noticeably affects results, and suggests open-source LLMs can serve as cost-efficient evaluators in the Vietnamese context. Score tables were not retrieved. — [ACL Anthology 2025.acl-long.563](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.563)
- Viettel AI trained VT-Super-120B-A12B, a "sovereign" Vietnamese model with 120B parameters built on NVIDIA Nemotron open models. Viettel claims stronger Vietnamese without degrading English (reported 4 Jun 2026). Another Viettel Nemotron-based model has 30B+ parameters and a 1M-token context; it is unclear whether it is the same model. — [Thị trường Tài chính Tiền tệ](https://thitruongtaichinhtiente.vn/viettel-huan-luyen-thanh-cong-mo-hinh-ai-chu-quyen-tieng-viet-120-ty-tham-so-dat-hieu-suat-thuoc-nhom-dan-dau-cung-quy-mo-83392.html); [Báo Hải quân](https://baohaiquanvietnam.vn/tin-tuc/viettel-huan-luyen-mo-hinh-ai-chu-quyen-tieng-viet-voi-120-ty-tham-so); [VnReview: Viettel AI ecosystem](https://vnreview.vn/threads/he-sinh-thai-ai-cua-viettel-gom-nhung-san-pham-nao.88727/)
- Zalo joined the Âu Lạc AI Alliance (June 2025) to co-develop Vietnamese LLMs. — [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/zalo-hien-ke-cho-lien-minh-ai-dau-tien-cua-viet-nam-20250625121021749.htm)
- VinAI announced PhởGPT as an open-source Vietnamese LLM research project in 2023 (older). — [SGGP](https://www.sggp.org.vn/phogpt-mo-ra-nhieu-ung-dung-sang-tao-post717280.html)
- Generic UX-writing guidance (e.g., Grafana) recommends a conversational tone that is less casual than texting a friend, and SAP recommends consistent terms. These are not Vietnam-specific. — [Grafana Writers' Toolkit](https://grafana.com/docs/writers-toolkit/write/style-guide/ux-writing/); [SAP design system](https://www.sap.com/design-system/hcm/getting-started/ux-writing/about-ux-writing/usage)

### Inferences
- **Native Vietnamese copy, not machine-translated copy, is a cheap differentiator.** Global AI tools mostly ship English-only dashboards or literal translations. A VN founder can write natural microcopy, VN-specific templates (hóa đơn, báo giá, Zalo message scripts, công văn) and a Vietnamese error vocabulary.
- **Model routing.** VMLU shows prompt design matters a lot for Vietnamese, and local sovereign models (Viettel) are emerging. A product that offers "Vietnamese-optimized prompts" and an optional local-model mode (data never leaves VN) can claim both quality and compliance. Benchmark claims should cite VMLU scores, which still need retrieving.

### Gaps
- No sourced guidance on Vietnamese pronoun choice in UI. My unverified practitioner lead: consumer apps (Shopee, Grab, MoMo) mostly use "bạn"; banks and government use "Quý khách"; sales and support chats on Zalo commonly use "anh/chị" for the customer and "em" for the staff member. Using "bạn" in B2B support with older shop owners can read as disrespectful, while "mày/tao" is never acceptable.
- No sourced data on diacritics handling (e.g., search must match both "hoa don" and "hóa đơn"; Telex/VNI input quirks; NFC vs NFD Unicode normalization). These are known engineering pitfalls but unsourced here.
- No sourced evidence on how VN users react to machine-translated interfaces.
- VMLU leaderboard scores for GPT-4o/GPT-5, Claude, Qwen, SeaLLM, Viettel and Zalo models were not retrieved.
