## 1. Vietnam scam landscape 2025–2026: scale, top scam types, who gets hit, which channels

### Takeaway
Official Vietnamese figures are about 4,200 online-fraud cases in 2025, with losses of roughly VND 5,000–8,000 billion (sources disagree), and about VND 40,000 billion (~US$1.5B) across 2020–2025. Survey estimates are far higher. Reported cases fell in H1 2026 (959 cases, ~VND 1.5 trillion). The main patterns:
- impersonation of police/VNeID/social-insurance officials, which moves victims to Zalo and has them install APK malware;
- investment and job/"task" scams, often run from Cambodian compounds;
- deepfake video and voice calls from hijacked accounts of relatives;
- spoofed brandname SMS sent from fake BTS (cell-tower) devices.
Phone calls are the top first-contact channel in Asia. Zalo, with about 81M monthly users, is where conversations move after first contact.

- The Ministry of Public Security groups online fraud into four families: impersonation, romance, investment, and malware distribution (Deputy Minister Phạm Thế Tùng, Oct 2025). Victims are told their ID or household data is "out of sync" and threatened with account locks or fines. They are then asked to add the caller on Zalo and use two phones to "authenticate". - **Deepfakes:** the National Cybersecurity Association (NCA) forecast more deepfake impersonation of acquaintances, leaders and officials in 2026 — [Vietbao](https://vietbao.vn/toi-pham-mang-se-dung-nhieu-cong-nghe-deepfake-de-lua-dao-trong-nam-2026-578655.html). **Who is most affected**
- Vietnam has about 16.1M people aged 60+ (over 16% of the population). ### Inferences
- Typical scam flow: first contact by call, SMS or spoofed brandname SMS, then the conversation moves to Zalo, then an APK or link, then the victim makes a transfer to a mule account. A checker can step in at four artifacts: the phone number, the link or APK, the bank account number, and the message or screenshot. The bank account number is the last chance before money moves, and only about 1% of victims recover everything.
- Scams escalate fast: about two-thirds happen within 24 hours of first contact. Speed and being reachable inside Zalo therefore matter more than depth of analysis.
- Scripts change monthly (VNeID-for-child, BHXH, wedding invite, đăng kiểm). A "current scam scripts" feed matched to the user's message ("this matches the 'update VNeID for your child' script police warned about on [date]") is a credible, low-cost differentiator.
- Elderly victims comply out of fear for their children. Verdict copy aimed at them should calm that fear ("Công an không bao giờ gọi điện yêu cầu… Con cháu bác không bị liên lụy gì") rather than only say "lừa đảo".

## 2. Existing tools in Vietnam and their UX; what is missing or frustrating

### Takeaway
Vietnam already has:
- a community nonprofit (Chống Lừa Đảo): website, browser extension, app, Telegram group, and since January 2026 an AI chatbot inside Zalo that reads screenshots;
- an association-run app (nTrust, from the NCA): database of over 1M records for phone numbers, accounts, links, QR codes and fake apps, plus call blocking;
- a government trust badge for websites (tinnhiemmang.vn, about 2,763 sites);
- the central bank's SIMO system, which warns or blocks at the moment of transfer: over 5.6M warnings by 28/9/2026, but only about 34% of warned customers stopped.
The gaps are not about features. No single public lookup covers SIMO or A05 account lists. Results are lookup-only ("not in database") with weak explanation. Coverage of new numbers is thin. On iOS, users must dig into Settings. There is no family loop, and no consumer deepfake checker exists in Vietnamese.

### Cited Findings
**Chống Lừa Đảo (CLĐ, chongluadao.vn): nonprofit founded by ex-hacker Ngô Minh Hiếu ("Hiếu PC")**
- The browser extension runs on Chrome, Firefox, Cốc Cốc, Edge and Brave and uses AI, machine learning and community-reported data (mechanism page dated 26/3/2025, authored by Ngô Minh Hiếu) — [chongluadao.vn/posts/mechanism](https://chongluadao.vn/posts/mechanism); [Quantrimang how-to](https://quantrimang.com/cong-nghe/cach-dung-tien-ich-chongluadao-179047)
- AI.Chongluadao.vn launched 28/4/2025 with a chatbot and AI modes. Traffic rose 5x the day after launch — [Thanh Niên](https://thanhnien.vn/luong-truy-cap-website-chongluadao-tang-gap-5-lan-sau-1-ngay-ra-mat-185250428143917381.htm); [Báo Gia Lai](https://baogialai.com.vn/cuu-hacker-hieu-pc-ra-mat-website-chong-lua-dao-co-gi-dang-chu-y-post205944.html). By mid-2025 it offered a website, extension, mobile app and advisory chatbot. Users can look up links, report sites, and get guidance on what to do after losing money. The search summary attributed this to a June 2025 article; the exact URL among [Thanh Niên](https://thanhnien.vn/4-thanh-nien-tao-cong-cu-chong-lua-dao-truc-tuyen-bang-ai-185250421154003719.htm) and [Báo Xây dựng](https://baoxaydung.vn/tri-tue-nhan-tao-ai-cua-nhom-sinh-vien-viet-nam-bat-bai-lua-dao-mang-192250517194630516.htm) is uncertain.

### Inferences
- **Being inside Zalo is no longer unique.** CLĐ already runs a Zalo chatbot that reads screenshots. A new checker must win on details:
  - first-reply speed;
  - one verdict that covers all artifacts (link, number, account, screenshot, voice);
  - explaining why, in plain Vietnamese;
  - pointing to the official script ("matches the police warning of 24/10");
  - family features;
  - a shareable result card.
- **SIMO's 34% stop rate (1.9M of 5.6M)** means about two-thirds of warned customers continue. Some warnings are false positives and some are ignored. Generic bank warnings at the moment of payment underperform. A checker used *before* the bank app (when the account number first appears in chat) gets more time and context. A tiered verdict (block / strong warning / caution) mirrors the KienlongBank/ABBank logic users already see.
- **No public lookup exists for SIMO/A05 lists.** A consumer checker cannot honestly claim "kiểm tra với dữ liệu Ngân hàng Nhà nước" unless it gets a partnership. It should state its data sources plainly to avoid the overclaiming that hurts trust.
- **"Not in database" ≠ safe.** nTrust guides admit new numbers slip through. Copy should say "Chưa có báo cáo, nhưng [n] dấu hiệu đáng ngờ trong tin nhắn…", never "An toàn".
- **Lookalike domains (chongluadao.info) and fake "anti-scam" services exist,** including the well-documented "recovery scam" pattern of impersonating police who promise to get money back ([LSVN](https://lsvn.vn/tiep-tuc-canh-giac-chieu-tro-mao-danh-cong-an-ho-tro-lay-lai-tien-bi-lua-dao-a148538.html)). A checker should publish one official Zalo OA, domain and app ID, and say plainly that it never asks for money, OTPs or remote access.
- **Deepfake checking for consumers in Vietnamese is effectively unserved.** This is the clearest feature gap, but see section 3 on accuracy limits.

### Gaps
- canhbao.khonggianmang.gov.vn: no search results; its current status and UX are unknown. The 2025 transfer of the Authority of Information Security to the Ministry of Public Security, and any resulting rebranding of NCSC tools, could not be confirmed.
- App-store review complaint patterns for nTrust, CLĐ, Truecaller VN and Whoscall VN could not be gathered (fetch blocked, search budget exhausted).
- Viettel/VNPT/MobiFone spam-call apps, branded-caller-ID services, and Truecaller's Vietnam user numbers: no data found.
- Whether Zalo shows in-chat scam warnings for strangers (beyond "Báo xấu") was not confirmed. Meta/Messenger scam warnings in Vietnam were not researched (search budget).
- CLĐ usage numbers (reports, MAU, chatbot volume) are not published in sources found.

## 3. Global comparables: UX details, confidence communication, false positives (scam checkers and AI-media detectors)

### Takeaway
Global consumer scam checkers have converged on: free use; a chat-bot inside the messenger you already use; screenshot input; a hedged three-part verdict ("likely a scam / not / can't tell") plus next steps; and family-admin protection. Monetisation comes from bank and telco distribution. Deepfake detectors still produce many false alarms on real-world content: false-alarm rates of 5–18% on images and up to 66% on video for some tools in a 2025 study. The free TrueMedia.org service closed in January 2025 because of cost. C2PA provenance is spreading (Pixel 10 signs every photo by default) but most media still carries no credential.

### Cited Findings
**Scam checkers**
- **Bitdefender Scamio:** free AI chatbot on the web, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and Discord. Users add it as a WhatsApp contact via QR code or link, then paste text, upload an image, send a link, or *describe the situation*. It returns "a verdict within seconds, alongside recommendations" — [Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/consumer/scamio); [Bitdefender blog](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/am-i-being-scammed-finding-out-has-never-been-easier); [SecurityBrief AU (WhatsApp in Australia)](https://securitybrief.com.au/story/bitdefender-launches-ai-scam-detector-scamio-on-whatsapp-in-australia); [gHacks (2023)](https://www.ghacks.net/2023/12/15/scam-or-not-bitdefenders-scamio-ai-promises-to-have-the-answer/)
- **Norton Genie (Gen Digital):** free, opened to the public in January 2024. Users upload a screenshot or paste the text of a message, email, website or social post. It says "whether a message is likely a scam or not". If it cannot tell, it says so and gives next steps. The App Store listing notes iOS 15+ and English only. This information is from 2023–24 and may be outdated — [Gen newsroom](https://newsroom.gendigital.com/2023-07-25-Introducing-Norton-Genie-Real-Time-AI-powered-Scam-Detection-at-Your-Fingertips); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6448706515)
- **ScamAdviser (now owned by Gogolook):** trust score from 1 to 100 built from 40+ signals; positive signals are weighed against negative ones and no single factor decides. Published bands: 1–20 "Very Likely Unsafe", 21–40 "Likely Unsafe", 41–60 "Caution Recommended", 61–80 "Likely Safe", 81–100 "Very Likely Safe". ScamAdviser calls the score "a risk indicator, not a verdict" — [ScamAdviser explainer](https://www.scamadviser.com/articles/scamadviser-algorithm-explainer); [FAQ](https://www.scamadviser.com/faq). On-device Gemini Nano checks unfamiliar links using real-time web signals (Pixel 10+, Galaxy S26 and others). The cloud tier supports Pixel 6+, Galaxy S24+ and other AICore devices. Warnings appear **inside the notification**, and the feature is being extended to third-party messaging apps through OEMs (Honor, Motorola, OPPO, OnePlus, realme, vivo, Xiaomi). It cannot block WhatsApp calls because of encryption — [Mothership (2023)](https://mothership.sg/2023/02/new-scamshield-bot-whatsapp-telegram/); [Goody Feed](https://goodyfeed.com/scamshield-bot-whatsapp-scam/); [ask.gov.sg](https://ask.gov.sg/scamshield/questions/cm6x7i44000ky59w5lpzrdj60)

**AI-media / deepfake detectors**
- **"Fit for Purpose? Deepfake Detection in the Real World"** (arXiv 2510.16556; tested on real political deepfakes, Sept 2025 snapshot):
  - On images, only 2 tools exceeded 90% accuracy and 4 exceeded 0.90 AUC. Most tools had false-alarm rates above 10%.

### Inferences
- **Verdict wording norm:** leading tools never say "safe". They use "likely / unlikely / can't tell" plus next steps (Genie), or five probability bands (ScamAdviser). A Vietnamese equivalent: "Rất có thể lừa đảo / Có dấu hiệu đáng ngờ / Chưa đủ dữ liệu để kết luận / Chưa thấy dấu hiệu lừa đảo". Always add "việc nên làm ngay".
- **For AI media, a binary "giả/thật" is not defensible.** False-alarm rates of 5–66% are far too high for real-world video. Better approach:
  - show "dấu hiệu" (signals) with visual evidence;
  - say what the tool *cannot* tell;
  - always pair the result with a non-technical check, such as calling back on a known number or asking a family "secret question";
  - read C2PA credentials when present (they can positively prove capture), and never treat their absence as evidence of fakery.
- **Cost risk:** TrueMedia closed because free deepfake checking was too expensive. A free Vietnamese checker needs a cheap triage path: hash and dedupe repeat media, as Cofacts does for repeat forwards, and run heavy video models only on demand.
- **Dedupe of viral items** (Cofacts-style) is both a speed win and a cost win: a fake "cảnh sát" video forwarded by 10,000 people should get an instant cached verdict.
- **Family admin** (Truecaller) and **guardian alerts** are now standard globally; no Vietnamese tool found offers them.
- **Google will push Gemini scam warnings into notifications** on Xiaomi, OPPO and vivo phones. A standalone checker's edge is then Vietnamese-specific knowledge (VNeID/BHXH scripts, Vietnamese bank account formats, Zalo context), bank-account checks, and family loops. Google does not do these.

- Hive, Sightengine, Sensity and Hiya consumer UX (how confidence percentages are shown, free tiers) were not researched; the search budget ran out.
- Norton Genie's 2025–2026 status (still standalone? folded into Norton apps?) and Gen Digital's newer "Scam Guardian" branding were not confirmed.
- No head-to-head accuracy data for voice-clone (audio deepfake) detection on phone-quality audio, or for Vietnamese speech.
- No 2025–2026 usage numbers for ScamShield, Scamio or Genie.

## 4. Behaviour: what makes people check before acting, why warnings get ignored, wording for elderly users, children helping parents

### Takeaway
People ignore warnings that look the same every time, arrive mid-task, or have cried wolf before. Even Vietnam's SIMO bank warnings stop only about a third of warned transfers. The checks that work happen early, inside the channel where the scam is unfolding (forward to a bot). They are built around the family: MyGoPen began as a tool for a founder's mother-in-law, and Truecaller lets an adult child end a scam call for a parent. Older Vietnamese victims are driven by fear for their children. That points to reassurance-first wording and a one-tap "ask a family member" action.

### Cited Findings
- **Habituation:** users "routinely ignore security warnings". A warning that looks identical every time becomes effectively invisible, and a bank that reuses one message format creates "an invisible message" — [Campus Technology (BYU study)](https://campustechnology.com/articles/2014/11/20/research-people-ignore-security-warnings-through-habit.aspx); [BioCatch](https://www.biocatch.com/blog/scam-warnings-customer-education)
- **Timing:** warnings shown mid-task are dismissed. Better moments are natural pauses, such as while a page loads — [Softpedia (BYU)](https://news.softpedia.com/news/people-ignore-security-warnings-because-they-come-at-bad-times-507531.shtml)
- **False positives desensitise users:** participants ignored warnings because most previous ones had turned out to be false positives — [Krol et al., UCL](https://discovery-pp.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1389027/1/KrolWarnings-CameraReady.pdf)
- **Victims' reasoning:** some thought the warning was generic and not about their case, or that it was a platform bug — [PMC 9317489](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9317489/)
- **Vietnam bank warning effectiveness:** 1.9M of 5.6M SIMO-warned customers stopped or cancelled, about 34% — [CafeF](https://cafef.vn/hon-19-trieu-luot-khach-hang-dung-giao-dich-sau-canh-bao-gian-lan-18826100811053583.chn)
- **Speed of scams:** about two-thirds of scams happen within 24 hours of first contact — [GASA](https://gasa.org/knowledge-base/blog/new-study-reveals-63-of-southeast-asians-experienced-scams-in-past-year)
- **Recovery is rare:** 1% of Vietnamese victims recovered all funds — [VietnamPlus](https://en.vietnamplus.vn/vietnamese-loss-1623-billion-usd-to-online-scams-post276711.vnp)
- **Elderly drivers in Vietnam:** fear of getting children into trouble plus unfamiliarity with technology account for 50% of elderly-victim cases (HCMC police). Loneliness and adult children with little time are also cited — [Sức khỏe & Đời sống](https://suckhoedoisong.vn/chuyen-gia-suc-khoe-tam-than-nguoi-cao-tuoi-bi-lua-vai-trieu-dong-cung-co-the-bi-sang-chan-tam-ly-169260616142735122.htm); [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/khoang-trong-khien-nguoi-gia-de-sap-bay-lua-dao-5090033.html)
- **Familiar official wording in Vietnam:** the police slogan "4 không, 2 phải". The four "don'ts": không sợ hãi (don't panic), không tham lam (don't be greedy), không kết bạn với người lạ (don't befriend strangers), không chuyển khoản (don't transfer). The two "musts": phải cảnh giác (stay alert), phải liên hệ công an khi nghi ngờ (contact police when in doubt). This is the Bắc Giang police version; other localities' wording may differ — [Thương hiệu & Công luận](https://thuonghieucongluan.com.vn/cong-an-bac-giang-canh-bao-phuong-thuc-thu-doan-lua-dao-chiem-doat-tai-san-a225102.html). A05 and Google published a handbook, "Chung tay phòng, chống lừa đảo Online" — [CTU PDF](https://ssc.ctu.edu.vn/images/upload/2025hd/Cm_nang_Chung_tay_phng_chng_la_o_Online.pdf)
- **Wording that hedges responsibly:** ABBank states that SIMO, NAPAS and A05 warnings "không mặc nhiên khẳng định người nhận là đối tượng lừa đảo" (do not by themselves assert the recipient is a scammer) — [Soha](https://soha.vn/tu-nay-ngan-hang-se-chu-dong-chan-giao-dich-neu-khach-hang-chuyen-tien-den-cac-tai-khoan-sau-198260920094151362.htm)
- **Forward-to-bot and intergenerational care:** MyGoPen started to protect the founder's mother-in-law and grew to about 2,000 inquiries a day, mostly from older LINE users — [Taiwan Insight](https://taiwaninsight.org/2023/03/31/three-musketeers-against-mis-disinformation-assessing-citizen-led-fact-checking-practices-in-taiwan/). Academic work frames the bot as "intergenerational care" — [U. Chicago journal](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/730621)
- **Family control:** Truecaller lets an admin remotely end a flagged scam call on a relative's Android phone — [TechCrunch](https://www.techcrunch.com/2025/12/09/truecaller-now-lets-users-protect-households-from-scam-calls/)
- **Deepfake-call behavioural tells used in Vietnamese advice:** a "weak signal" excuse to end the call quickly; mismatched lip-sync; flat voice. Recommended actions: check the account holder's name before transferring, and call back on another channel — [Kenh14](https://kenh14.vn/chuyen-gia-hieu-pc-chi-5-cach-nhan-dien-cuoc-goi-lua-dao-deepfake-21526051218391675.chn); [VietnamPlus](https://www.vietnamplus.vn/canh-bao-thu-doan-loi-dung-cong-nghe-deepfake-de-lua-dao-chiem-doat-tai-san-post1023564.vnp)
- Ashamed victims often feel worse because family had warned them — [Wymoo](https://www.wymoo.com/blog/online-dating-scam-victims-ignore-the-warnings/) (lower-quality source)

### Inferences (micro-differentiators backed by the above)
- **Warn at a natural pause.** Check when the account number or link first appears in chat (forwarded to the bot), not at the bank's "Confirm" button. Avoid alert fatigue: no generic banners, and every verdict should cite *this* message's specific red flags. This answers the "it's generic, not about me" failure.
- **Vary the warning surface.** Use specific scripts and quotes ("Tin nhắn yêu cầu bạn 'dùng 2 điện thoại để xác thực' — đây là kịch bản giả công an VNeID") so the warning never becomes "invisible".
- **Keep false positives low and be honest about uncertainty.** False alarms train people to ignore warnings. A "Chưa đủ dữ liệu" tier protects credibility.
- **Reassurance-first copy for elders.** Address the fear directly: "Công an thật không gọi điện bắt chuyển tiền. Con cháu bác không bị liên lụy." Large type, a single primary action, and voice playback of the verdict for low-literacy users (inference; not tested in sources).
- **"Hỏi con/cháu" button.** Sends the verdict card plus the original screenshot to a chosen family member on Zalo in one tap. The child can reply "Đừng chuyển" right in the thread. This copies MyGoPen's origin and Truecaller's admin model, adapted to Zalo.
- **Guardian mode:** an adult child registers a parent. When the parent checks something flagged high-risk, or checks a bank account at all, the child gets an alert. Explicit consent and no message reading, following Truecaller's privacy framing.
- **Speed is the main feature.** Scams resolve within 24 hours and often within one call. Aim for a first answer in seconds (a cached or known hit), then a richer follow-up.
- **"Đã lỡ chuyển tiền?" path.** With only 1% recovery, the first-hour script matters most: call your bank's hotline to freeze the transfer, report to police, and do not pay "recovery" services impersonating police.
- **Use the familiar slogans** ("4 không, 2 phải") as a closing line, since older users already know the police wording.

### Gaps
- No Vietnamese user research on wording that works for elderly users, on what share of adult children manage their parents' phones, or on whether elders accept guardian monitoring.
- UK Confirmation of Payee / tailored-warning effectiveness evidence was not retrieved (search budget exhausted).
- No A/B data on verdict wording (e.g., "likely scam" vs "scam") from any vendor.

## 5. Business models, data partnerships, government cooperation, and Vietnamese data-protection rules

### Takeaway
The proven model is a free consumer tool that builds data and brand, with revenue from banks, telcos and platforms. Gogolook, at about NT$1.05B revenue in 2025 and 70% from anti-fraud, sells certified-number services and bank-paid Premium distribution. In Vietnam the strongest data sits with the state (A05 lists, SIMO for 21 institutions, NCA's 1M+ record database). Vietnam's new Personal Data Protection Law (in force 1/1/2026), with Decree 356/2025, sets fines of up to 5% of revenue. Community blacklists of phone numbers and account numbers therefore need a lawful basis, verification and a dispute path.

### Inferences
- **B2B wedge in Vietnam:** only 21 institutions are on SIMO, so many e-wallets, marketplaces, smaller banks, fintech lenders and telcos sit outside that data loop. A checker that aggregates community reports with scam-script and link intelligence could sell API checks on account numbers, phone numbers and links to non-SIMO players and marketplaces. The partner list is inferred; the count of non-participating institutions was not verified.
- **Distribution via banks or telcos** (the Whoscall/CTBC/BPI pattern) is a realistic route: a Vietnamese bank could bundle "kiểm tra trước khi chuyển" or a family-guardian feature for its customers.
- **Government cooperation is a trust signal**, not just a data source. Being listed on tinnhiemmang.vn and sending reports to A05/NCA, with the verdict saying so, borrows state credibility. Overclaiming access to SIMO/A05 would be a liability.
- **Privacy-by-design as a differentiator under the new law:**
  - store hashes of phone and account numbers;
  - show reports only in aggregate ("12 báo cáo trong 30 ngày, 3 đã xác minh");
  - never show reporters' identities;
  - offer a public dispute or appeal path for wrongly flagged accounts, since mule accounts are often real people's accounts;
  - process media without retaining it.
  These choices also protect against defamation claims.
- **Cost control** (TrueMedia lesson): cache viral items; tier compute (fast heuristics first, heavy models on request); consider an enterprise deepfake API (KYC, video-call verification for banks) to subsidise the free consumer side.

### Gaps
- I found no explicit legal provision in the PDP Law or Decree 356 that permits or exempts *private* anti-fraud data sharing (e.g., community blacklists, or sharing between a checker and banks). Legal review needed.
- No information on whether SIMO or the A05 list will open to non-bank third parties, or on any SBV circular formalising automatic blocking in 2026 (search summaries found bank-level policies, not a circular number).
- No data on what Vietnamese banks or e-wallets pay for anti-fraud APIs, or on local B2B competitors (e.g., Viettel Cyber Security and VNPT anti-fraud offerings were not researched).
- Business models of CLĐ and nTrust (funding, sponsors, any API offering) were not found.
